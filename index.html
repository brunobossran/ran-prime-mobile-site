Questions

Is this the same as the PC server?

No. This is a new, separate server, and everyone starts at level 1. Looking for the PC version? Visit ep7.ranonlineofficial.com.

When can I play?

We're building and testing now. The official announcement and test dates will come first on our Facebook page. Join the test list and we'll email you when testing opens.

How will I get the test?

Android: we'll add your Google account to our Google Play test and email you a link. You install the game from the Play Store like any other app.

iPhone and iPad: we'll send an invite to your Apple ID email. Install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open the invite to install the game.

Which phones will be supported?

We're testing on a wide range of Android phones and iPhones, including older models. Add your phone model when you join the list so we can include it.

Is it free?

Yes. RAN - Prime Dev Mobile is free to play.

Will there be paid guilds or bots?

No paid guilds. Anti-bot and anti-cheat run from day one.

Is this the official RAN Online?

No. RAN - Prime Dev Mobile is made by Prime Developments, an independent team.

How do I contact you?

Email support@ranonlineofficial.com or send us a message on our Facebook page.
